# Trademarks and project identity

No source licence grants permission to claim that a product, service, module or
organisation is officially endorsed by ANWOR or Ivy.

The names, logos and badges used for `ANWOR Official` and `ANWOR Verified` are
reserved for artefacts that have received the corresponding version-specific
approval. Community projects may state factual compatibility but must not use
official branding in a misleading way.

Final trademark wording and logo-usage rules require approval before public
launch.
