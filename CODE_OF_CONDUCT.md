# Community conduct

Ivy welcomes respectful technical disagreement and people with different levels
of experience.

Participants must not harass, threaten, discriminate against, dox or deliberately
humiliate others. Critique ideas and evidence, not people. Do not publish private
security reports, personal information or confidential business material.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation when
necessary. Before public launch, ANWOR must select an approved standard code of
conduct and publish a private reporting address. This interim text is not a
substitute for that final policy.
