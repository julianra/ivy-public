# Module categories and ownership

[Nederlands](../../nl/modules/module-categories.md) | **English**

<!-- section: official -->
## ANWOR Official

Official modules are released under ANWOR control with approved provenance,
package identity and a stated support position. The protected system-module
licence is not final. Official status applies only to an exact release.

<!-- section: verified -->
## ANWOR Verified

An independently owned module may receive a version-specific review. Approval is
bound to module identity, version and package digest. A modified build or later
version is not automatically verified.

<!-- section: external -->
## External modules

Developers retain rights in modules they independently create. They may offer
them free or commercially, proprietary or open source, subject to the rights
they hold, applicable law and third-party terms. Unreviewed modules must not
claim official approval.

<!-- section: ownership -->
## Contributions to shared code

Ownership and licensing for a developer's separate module are distinct from
contributions to this shared Developer Kit. Accepted Developer Kit contributions
are Apache-2.0 and certified under DCO 1.1; no CLA is used.

<!-- section: rewards -->
## Recognition and rewards

ANWOR may separately agree attribution, credits, payment or another reward for
accepted work. There is no automatic entitlement and no contribution creates a
payment promise.
