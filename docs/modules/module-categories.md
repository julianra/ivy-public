# Module categories

## ANWOR Official

An official module is released under ANWOR control with approved provenance,
package identity and support statement. Official status applies to a concrete
version, not every fork or future build.

## ANWOR Verified

A third-party module may receive version-specific review. Verification does not
transfer ownership to ANWOR and does not turn later versions into verified
artefacts. The exact checks and signed metadata are not yet final.

## Unverified external modules

External developers may distribute original modules for free or for payment,
open or closed source, subject to applicable law, third-party rights and Ivy
compatibility. They must not imply official approval.
